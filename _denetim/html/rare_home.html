SÜGISBALL (2007) AKA AUTUMN BALL

Set in Estonia before the Soviet stranglehold was shattered, filmmaker Veiko Öunpuu’s award-winning drama brings together a group of disparate — and desperate –characters from a local housing complex. A writer, an architect, a single mother, her young daughter, a barber and a nightclub doorman make up this collection of lost souls who bleakly face the future while reaching blindly for some form of connection.

TÜHIRAND (2006) AKA EMPTY

TVÁŘ (1973) AKA THE FACE

In three minutes, the entire human life unfolds in the transformations of one face – from birth to death. Without the use of camera movement, in a static shot, with minimal music, an image of growth and decay is created. For the artist Jiří Anderle, this was the only and unique collaboration with an animated film.

MOC OSUDU (1969) AKA THE POWER OF DESTINY

An ironic tale pondering whether we write our own destiny in advance. Palmistry, fate, surrealist imagery, and endless debates about how to glimpse the future come together in a film that also fulfills Brdečka’s long-standing desire to bring artist Miroslav Štěpánek’s work to the screen. The film reflects Brdečka’s skepticism toward fortune-telling and mysticism.
